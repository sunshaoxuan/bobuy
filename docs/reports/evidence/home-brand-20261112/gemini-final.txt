### Visible Defects in the Changed Brand Asset

#### 1. Visual Centering & Optical Balance
* **Region:** Lower row (`bobuy.cc`) relative to the upper lockup.
* **Defect:** While `bobuy.cc` is mathematically centered relative to the total bounding width (from the far-left edge of the red badge to the far-right stroke of `败`), it is **optically off-center**. Because the solid red badge has significantly more visual weight and solid surface area than the open strokes of the Chinese characters, the domain appears pulled too far to the right and visually unanchored.

#### 2. Visual Hierarchy & Contrast
* **Region:** Text color and stroke weight of `bobuy.cc`.
* **Defect:** 
  * The Chinese glyphs are heavy, dense black, and the badge is a high-saturation solid red. 
  * In contrast, `bobuy.cc` is rendered in a thin, muted grey/slate tone. This creates a disjointed third tier of styling rather than a unified lockup, making the domain feel washed out and visually secondary to the point of being ignored.

#### 3. Mobile Readability & Rendering Quality
* **Region:** Letterforms in `bobuy.cc` (especially the `.`, `c`, and `y` descender).
* **Defect:** Due to the thin font weight, small scale, and anti-aliasing/compression, the domain text suffers from visible blur and edge degradation. The period (`.`) and inner counters of the letters (`b`, `o`, `c`) close up, making it difficult to read quickly on mobile screens.

#### 4. Spacing
* **Region:** Vertical gap between the upper row (baseline of `宝宝败` / bottom curve of red badge) and the top of `bobuy.cc`.
* **Defect:** The vertical gap separating the brand mark from `bobuy.cc` is disproportionately large compared to the compact horizontal gap between the red badge and the first `宝`. This excessive gap breaks cohesion, making the domain float as a detached element rather than reading as part of an integrated lockup.

#### 5. Cropping & Outer Margins
* **Region:** Outer perimeter of the canvas (all four boundaries).
* **Defect:** The asset is cropped with almost zero safe-zone padding:
  * **Top & Left:** The outer radius of the red rounded rectangle nearly touches the canvas borders.
  * **Right:** The rightmost flick of `败` borders directly on the edge.
  * **Bottom:** The descender of the `y` in `bobuy.cc` touches the bottom trim.
  * Without protective margin, clipping or awkward edge collisions will occur when placed inside web containers or header bars.

### Summary Checklist Against Requirements

* **Hierarchy:** Weak; domain is underpowered due to low-contrast color and thin stroke weight compared to the dominant red badge and black lettering.
* **Visual Centering:** Geometrically centered across the full width, but optically unbalanced due to the heavy mass of the red badge on the left.
* **Spacing:** Vertical separation under the main brand is too loose relative to the tight inner spacing of the top elements.
* **Cropping:** Margins are overly tight on all outer edges (top, left, right, and bottom descender).
* **Mobile Readable Domain:** Poor legibility at reduced scales due to thin strokes, muted color, and compression blur (unlike the mobile text-only variant which avoids the icon-balance issue).
