#### Image 5: Phone Editor
* **Visible Defects:**
  * **Mobile Hover Tooltip Artifact (Card 1 bottom, annotated in pink):** A dark tooltip (`解除关联`) with a pointer arrow is displayed directly above the trash icon. On a touch device, hover-based tooltips either persist awkwardly upon tap or obstruct adjacent interactive elements.

  * Card 1 checkbox has a red circular outline, whereas Card 2 has a neutral gray border. It is uncertain from the visual whether this represents a form validation error state or an inspection overlay.
  * Rating row in this view (`★★★★½ 4.3 / 5`) does *not* wrap, contrasting with Image 4. This indicates layout divergence between preview cards and editor cards.
* **Missing Evidence:** Disabled vs. active tap interaction on `公开这一商品`.

#### Image 6: Phone Product Records
* **Visible Defects:**
  * **Structural Asymmetry / Missing Image Slot (Top-Left Card vs. Top-Right Card):** Top-left card (`抹茶套装`) completely omits the image area, starting immediately with text metadata. Top-right card (`鲜鸡胸肉 · 1kg`) includes a full-bleed photo header. This causes total vertical misalignment between the two columns for titles, tags, rating stars, prices, and footer actions.
  * **Touch Target Crowding (Top-Right Card, annotated in pink):** The action button `售价与调价` and the red trash icon sit immediately adjacent to each other with minimal horizontal separation, presenting a high misclick risk on touch screens.
* **Uncertainties:** Top-left card shows `尚未确认售价` without a large numerical price block, which further exacerbates row mismatch.
* **Missing Evidence:** Behavior when a card has an image placeholder instead of complete omission.
