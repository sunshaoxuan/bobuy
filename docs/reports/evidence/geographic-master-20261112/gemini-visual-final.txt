### 1. Visible Defects / Doubts

* **Touch target size for pagination (Mobile):** The pagination buttons (`<`, `1`, `2`, `...`, `10`, `>`) appear smaller than standard mobile touch target guidelines (44×44px / 48×48px) and are spaced closely together, which may increase miss-taps.
* **Long select option text truncation risk (Doubt):** In mobile view, `"南都留郡富士河口湖町 (JP-19430)"` and `"South Georgia & South Sandwich Islands (GS)"` fit tightly against the dropdown chevron. Verify that `text-overflow: ellipsis` is applied so even longer values (or viewports under 360px) do not collide with or slide under the right-aligned chevron.
* **Disabled select state legibility (Doubt):** In the empty/non-Japan state (English mobile), disabled dropdown placeholder text (`"Select a prefecture"`, `"Select a city/ward"`) displays low visual contrast against the greyed-out input background.
