### 1. Concrete Defects, Doubts & Missing Evidence

* **Defect 1: Content clipping & missing scroll clearance at bottom (Desktop 1440 - Image 1)**
  * The bottom border and padding of the "商品明细" section card are clipped directly behind the sticky bottom footer. The scroll container lacks sufficient `padding-bottom` (footer clearance), cutting off the card container boundary.
* **Defect 2: Harsh scroll cutoff / header separation seam (Mobile 320 - Image 4)**
  * When scrolled, content from the top section (`JPY` dropdown and card container) is abruptly sliced in half directly below the sticky title bar without a bottom border, elevation shadow, or backdrop mask to cleanly separate fixed vs. scrolled content.
* **Doubt: Mobile Touch Targets < 44px (Mobile 320 - Images 2, 3, 4)**
  * Buttons ("新增调价单", "刷新", "编辑", "审核生效", "撤销", and footer "关闭"/"保存草稿") visually scale to ~32px–38px in height, falling short of the recommended 44px minimum tap target standard for mobile touch accessibility.
* **Missing Evidence:**
  * No evidence of behavior when virtual keyboard is raised on 320px (potential obscuring by sticky footer).
  * No evidence of inline error/validation message presentation when required fields fail validation.

### 3. Actionable Blocking Fixes for UI Acceptance

1. **Header Scroll Divider (Mobile):** Add a subtle bottom border or shadow to the sticky drawer header container so scrolling elements do not look severed at the boundary.
2. **Scroll Container Padding (Desktop & Mobile):** Add bottom padding equal to or greater than the footer height (e.g., `pb-20` / `padding-bottom: 80px`) on the drawer body so the final card’s bottom border and spacing are fully visible above the sticky footer.
3. **Mobile Touch Heights:** Increase mobile button and interactive control heights to `min-height: 44px` on narrow screens to meet touch target standards.
