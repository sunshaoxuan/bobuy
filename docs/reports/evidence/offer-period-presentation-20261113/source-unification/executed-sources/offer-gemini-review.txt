### 1. Visible Defects

* **Awkward line wrapping of date phrases (narrow widths):** In narrower containers/cards (Image 2 & 4), the date string breaks abruptly across lines (e.g., `11月9日至` / `11月22日` or `2024年` / `1月2日至` / `2027年` / `12月30日`). Splitting a single date span across arbitrary token boundaries degrades readability for shoppers at a glance.
* **Overlap with critical card metadata:** When the hint pops open to the right on mobile cards (Image 2 & 4), it occludes vital header info (e.g., the product code `No.1840548` and heat score `🔥 1`) as well as underlying card imagery and titles.
* **Card boundary overflow & viewport collision risk:** On mobile two-column grids (Image 2), expanding strictly to the right causes the tooltip to protrude beyond the right boundary of the host card, leaving almost no buffer before the screen edge. On an extreme right-column card, a fixed rightward expansion will clip against the screen edge.
* **Multi-period stacking clutter (as tested in Image 4):** Even though synthetic, stacking multiple ranges vertically in an unconstrained right-floating popover creates a disproportionately tall container that obstructs the product card almost entirely.

---

### 2. Right-Side Placement Evaluation

* **Desktop/Wide view (Image 1):** Works reasonably well because the card has enough horizontal canvas (~200–250px+) to accommodate the badge, popover, and top tags without severe obstruction.
* **Narrow / 2-Column Mobile view (Image 2 & 4):** 
  * Placement directly to the right (`left: 100%`) within a narrow column (~160–180px per card) is **structurally fragile**. 
  * Because the "惠" badge is already pinned near the top-left of the image, projecting rightward leaves ~100–120px of width, which is insufficient for comfortable Chinese date strings without wrapping or overflowing the card boundary.
  * **Recommendation:** Keep rightward expansion for wide screens, but for narrow screens/mobile cards, either:
    1. Allow the tooltip to anchor below the badge (`placement: bottom-start`), or
    2. Expand rightward across full card width with an automatic flip / clamp (`max-width: calc(100% - margin)`) to avoid escaping the card or colliding with screen borders.

---

### 3. Readable Month/Day Wrapping & Wording

* **Wording clarity:**
  * The label `优惠期间:` (or concise `优惠期:`) is clear and universally understood by Chinese shoppers.
* **Wrapping rules (`white-space` & token grouping):**
  * Date ranges should never break inside a date component (e.g., separating the year and month/day, or breaking `11月` from `9日`).
  * If wrapping is necessary due to fixed container constraints, keep whole dates intact:
    * **Ideal single-line:** `11月9日 - 11月22日` (using an en-dash `–` or `至` without breaking).
    * **Acceptable 2-line wrap:**
      ```text
      优惠期间:
      11月9日 至 11月22日
      ```
      (The entire start-to-end date string remains together, or breaks cleanly before the connector `至` / `~`).
  * Current year should generally be omitted if it is within the current calendar year (as seen in Images 1–3), which saves substantial horizontal space and avoids unnecessary wrapping.

---

### 4. Clipping and Spacing Evaluation

* **Internal Spacing (Padding):**
  * The padding inside the tooltip (Image 1–3) is clean, but vertical padding is slightly tight relative to font line-height.
* **External Spacing & Clipping:**
  * If cards or list containers have `overflow: hidden`, any popover anchored strictly to the right will be clipped at the card edge unless rendered into a portal/floating-ui layer with high z-index.
  * The popover currently sits directly flush or with minimal gap from the badge; maintaining an 8px horizontal offset (`margin-left: 8px`) preserves clear visual association without crowding the badge.
