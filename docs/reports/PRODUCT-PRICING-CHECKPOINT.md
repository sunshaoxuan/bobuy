Implemented locally: independent ProductPriceAdjustment entity / V17 additive table, draft edit / explicit approval / cancellation and retained history endpoints under existing agent ownership; existing ProductSale remains the confirmed regular quote. Starts are inclusive and ends exclusive instants, entered/displayed explicitly in JST. Approval locks the product and rejects same-product/channel/currency overlaps while permitting adjacent intervals. Drafts do not change price. Unpriced, disabled-currency, wrong-currency or unsupported fulfillment products cannot activate adjustments. A pending/active adjustment prevents incompatible regular currency/unit edits. Product deletion is rejected when pricing history exists. No reference discount becomes a selling price or adjustment automatically.

ProductSaleService resolves effective prices for storefront products, recommendations and checkout. It falls back to the original regular quote before/after the interval and still respects regular availability and product visibility. Public quotes carry a full SHA256 price fingerprint; checkout verifies it with currency/revision at one server timestamp, so a stale cart requires review rather than silently charging a changed price. Existing carts without a fingerprint must refresh. New order-line JSON snapshots retain exact BigDecimal amount, regular amount, adjustment ID/channel and pricing timestamp; later price changes do not rewrite orders. The UI refreshes quotes at the next known boundary without automatically accepting a new cart price.

UI: existing StockMaster rows open Selling prices and adjustments. The drawer separates regular quote confirmation, adjustment drafts and history with exact JST intervals. Approval/cancellation requires explicit review. Recommendation cards/detail show effective selling price and active interval separately from the original source offer. Four-language UI/server errors are present. No new navigation module, security role, credential, translation configuration or inventory flow was introduced.
